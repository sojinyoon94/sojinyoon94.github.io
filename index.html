Research Interests

Bridging psychology and statistics, I apply advanced statistical modeling to uncover mechanisms underlying psychological problems and inform effective interventions.

  • Latent transition analysis

  • Multilevel analysis
  • Dynamic structural equation modeling (DSEM)
